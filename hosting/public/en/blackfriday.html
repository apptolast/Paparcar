Questions about the gift

Who can get it?

Only NEW accounts: ones created during this campaign that open the app for the first time after it started. If you already had Paparcar installed before the campaign, this offer is not for your account.

Do I need to enter a code?

No. The gift is granted automatically the first time your new account reaches the app's home screen. There is no code to type and no form to fill in.

What happens when the 3 months are over?

The app warns you before it ends. If you do nothing, you go back to the free plan: nothing is deleted, alerts and the extended history simply pause.

How long do I have to get it?

Until 1 December 2026 at 00:00 CET, or until all 2000 accounts in the pool are claimed, whichever comes first.
